tldr:
  - "Proposed a top-of-funnel plan for the school: five video formats, one video every day."
  - "Formats: AI news from the presenter's avatar, micro dramas, AI-generated skits, AI actor videos and repurposed recordings."
  - Designed the plan as a one-page infographic on the school's design system, so it could be pitched and reused.

context:
  problem: The school grows when more people discover it, long before any sales conversation. Discovery on social depends on showing up often and giving people more than one reason to stop scrolling. The account was posting good variety, but without a schedule, an owner or a way to tell which formats worked.
  business: Colaberry School helps learners build careers in data and AI. I work across marketing and the school as analyst and designer, and bring proposals to the weekly marketing and school management call.
  constraints:
    - A video every day from a small team.
    - The lead presenter's time is limited, so most formats had to work without him filming each day.
    - Everything had to stay on the school's brand and design system.
research:
  summary: Frequency and variety do different jobs. Posting daily keeps the account in front of the algorithm. Rotating formats shows which kind of video people actually share.
  insights:
    - The account was already posting all five kinds of content, just not on a plan.
    - The micro dramas hooked on everyday workplace tension, like who gets promoted first.
    - Existing long recordings could be cut into reels, so not every day needs new production.
process:
  summary: Sort what was already working into formats, give each a day, then staff it and measure it.
  steps:
    - title: Sort what was already live
      body: "I grouped recent posts into the kinds of video they were: AI news clips, micro dramas, AI-actor news parodies, skits with talking animals, and presenter clips with brand cards."
    - title: Five blocks, one per weekday
      body: "Each format became a block with its own day: AI with the presenter's avatar, micro dramas, AI-generated skits made in Google Flow, AI actor videos and repurposed recordings."
    - title: Make the plan easy to say yes to
      body: I designed the strategy as a one-page infographic on the school's design system, showing the five blocks feeding one daily video and the reach it aims for.
    - title: Staff the cadence
      body: A video editor joined the team, so a daily schedule became realistic rather than an extra load on design.
    - title: Measure per block
      body: The next step is tracking reach for each block separately, so the team scales the formats that travel and drops the ones that don't.
ai:
  summary: "Four of the five formats are AI-generated or AI-assisted: the presenter's avatar, AI actors, and skits generated from scripts in Google Flow. AI is what makes a daily cadence possible for a small team."
  evaluation: Reach tracked per block, once the daily cadence starts.
decisions:
  - decision: Rotate five formats instead of building one signature series.
    tradeoff: Harder to become known for one thing, and more production styles to keep on brand.
  - decision: "Put cadence ahead of polish: one video every day."
    tradeoff: A lower ceiling on any single video, in exchange for more chances to learn and to reach new people.
  - decision: Use the presenter's avatar and AI actors so output does not depend on anyone's filming schedule.
    tradeoff: AI-made presenters need careful judgment on tone and honesty, and a person checking every video.
  - decision: Measure reach per block before scaling any single format.
    tradeoff: Slower to double down on a winner, but the bet is based on data rather than taste.
outcomes:
  summary: These are outputs, not results yet. The strategy was proposed, a video editor joined the team, and all five kinds of content are already live on the school's Instagram. Reach per block will be measured once the daily cadence starts.
quote: Daily cadence gets you seen. Variety tells you what people actually want to watch.
reflection:
  summary: "Content can be designed like a product: clear formats, a schedule, an owner and a metric for each part."
  learnings:
    - A plan built from what is already working gets agreed faster than a plan built from scratch.
    - A cadence without a person to carry it is only a wish. Staffing was part of the design.
    - Next time I would set the reach metric for each format on day one, alongside the plan, not as a follow-up.
