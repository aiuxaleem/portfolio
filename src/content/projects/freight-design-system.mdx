## What it is

The design system for an AI freight platform that books, tracks and reroutes shipments across land, air and ocean. The client is not named here. It holds the brand foundations, 121 design tokens, 43 React components in seven groups, six templates and a four-screen control-tower UI kit, in light and dark themes.

## What people make with it

- **Product screens:** an app shell, a data table page and a settings page, plus the control-tower kit (dashboard, shipments, shipment detail, new quote).
- **Marketing:** a landing page template.
- **Presentations:** a 1920 by 1080 deck with seven slide types.
- **Mobile:** a tracking screen for the person receiving the shipment.

## How AI builds with it

The repository carries a Claude Code skill pack. A person writes the brief the way a product owner would; the skill makes Claude compose from the real components and tokens, write in the house voice and handle every state. A checker script then flags what should not ship: colours outside the palette, more than one orange action per view, icon buttons without labels and fields with only a placeholder. The two example pages in the gallery were built this way with no hand edits.

## How the product's own AI is designed

Every AI recommendation follows one pattern: the title is the action, then cost, impact and confidence as three numbers, a "why this" disclosure in the operator's language, one primary action that opens a review and never applies itself, an undo, and a feedback control. Confidence is always a number, never a colour. The six AI experience measures the system uses are adapted from Simran Agarwal's AI Product Designer Playbook.

## Who uses it

Colaberry's development team builds the platform's screens on it, and the landing page and deck templates give colleagues in sales and marketing a starting point that is already on brand.
