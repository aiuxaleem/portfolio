## What it is

The landing page for AIXcelerator by Colaberry. The product reads a company from its web address, proposes the roles it expects to find, and scores each one for how exposed it is to AI, then benchmarks the result against comparable companies. The page has one job: explain that in a few screens and get a leader to run the screen on their own company.

## How the page explains it

- **Three steps:** read your business, score routine against judgment, get your benchmark. Each step says what the visitor confirms or adjusts, because the model proposes and the person decides.
- **The benchmark as heat:** a grid of role tiles in three zones, green where judgment leads, amber in between and red where the work is routine. The key names each zone in words, so the colour is never the only signal.
- **Two layers, one scale:** a role layer that answers who is affected, and a task layer that answers where to spend the next AI build dollar, both scored 1 to 5.

## Design decisions

- **An analytical voice,** written for a chief executive: "honest scoring, not a sales deck". No inflated claims, and every number on the page describes how the screen works rather than promising an outcome.
- **One action,** Run my exposure screen, repeated at the top, in the header and at the close.
- **A dark, quiet palette** so the three exposure colours are the only strong colours on the page and read as data.
