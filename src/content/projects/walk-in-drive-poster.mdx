## What it is

A hiring poster for a three-day walk-in drive. It had to carry eleven facts, from role and skills to dates, venue and work location, and still be read at a glance on a phone.

## How it was built

1. Three rounds of exploration, twelve directions in all.
2. A bento grid won, because it was the only layout where every fact had a home.
3. One portrait master, adapted to landscape and square ads.
4. A UX-law audit of a late change took the layout from 6 to 9 out of 10.

## Result

The marketer ran the designs as Meta and LinkedIn ads, and around 200 to 300 people walked in over three days.
